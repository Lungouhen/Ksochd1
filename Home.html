About KSO Chandigarh

The Kuki Students' Organisation (KSO) Chandigarh is a dedicated student organization serving the Kuki community in Chandigarh. We provide support, networking opportunities, and promote cultural activities among students.

Our Mission

To unite Kuki students, promote academic excellence, preserve cultural heritage, and provide a platform for personal and professional development in Chandigarh.
